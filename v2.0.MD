## 5. India-first market stance + whale logic

`DONE` — `engine/v2/market_structure.py` studies daily OHLCV and never pretends to
see the order book.

- **Absorption** — volume z ≥ 2.0 with a range ≤ 0.6 × ATR: a passive desk is soaking up aggression.
- **Sliced execution / iceberg** — ≥ 3 overlapping same-direction closes on rising volume: the footprint of a parent order worked by an execution algo.
- **Liquidity sweep** — a wick through a 10-bar extreme that closes back inside: stops harvested, level defended.
- **Initiative aggression** — ≥ 2.5σ volume with a ≥ 1.2 × ATR body: a taker crossing the spread.
- **Wyckoff phase** — accumulation / markup / distribution / markdown from range position + volume trend + slope, with a crowd-psychology line.
- **Risk** — ATR(14), 1.5× ATR stop distance, swing highs/lows, prior-day levels.
- `DONE` Every study carries `limits` and `exchange_depth_available: False`. The desktop stance (Indian indices driven by domestic flow plus large external shocks, not by NASDAQ beta) is implemented by keeping the macro layer a *capped tilt* on top of GIFT/ADR/OI, not a foreign-index forecast.

## 6. Strategy lab

`DONE` — `engine/v2/strategy_lab.py`, output in **`strategies/`**.

- **Naming:** `Z2-<FAMILY>-<INDEX2><HASH4>`, alphanumeric, deterministic for a given parameter set.
- **Families (2.1 rework):** `BREAKOUT` (15/20/30-day high), `MAJOR` (40/55-day high), `PULLBACK` (a breakout that is not extended from the 20/50-day mean) and `TREND` (regime cross, hold until regime loss). All four express one measured mechanism — time-series momentum — at different horizons and filters; the six pattern families from 2.0 were removed because they measured negative-to-zero edge (see below).
- **Regime gate:** every entry must agree with a 200-day mean **and its slope over 20 bars** — longs only above a rising mean. A flat mean is not a trend.
- **Direction:** long-only by default. Measured across every family, short presets returned profit factors of **0.2–0.6** (2016–2026 is an upward-drifting index), so shorting is an explicit `direction_mode="long_short"` opt-in, not the default.
- **Confirmation close:** the signal bar must close in the directional half of its range (top 45% for longs).
- **Trade management:** initial 2×ATR stop, a **6×ATR chandelier trail** that only ratchets from closed-bar highs, a 400-bar safety time stop, and a regime-loss exit. No fixed R target: the measured data shows fixed targets cap the right tail that pays for the losses.
- **Risk posture:** 0.75% of equity risked per trade, 12 bps cost charged **per leg** (24 bps round trip).
- **History:** ten years. Three years leaves only ~500 usable bars behind a 200-day gate — too few to distinguish an edge from luck.
- **No look-ahead:** signal on the close, fill at the **next open**; if stop and target are in the same bar, the stop is assumed first. Signals are computed on the full series and only read from the test start, so an OOS slice still sees its 200-day mean.
- **Documents per strategy:** `strategies/<ID>.md` (thesis, rules, parameters, metrics, reproduction snippet) and `strategies/_@<ID>_, mistakes and P&L.md` (realised P&L, worst/best trades, measured mistakes, fix list).
- **Validation (new, and never part of the gate):** each strategy also reports a last-30% holdout and a five-fold consistency check (rules fixed, history split into contiguous folds). `validated` requires holdout PF ≥1.0 on ≥5 holdout trades with positive holdout P&L **and** ≥60% of folds positive. A strategy can pass the gate and still be labelled unvalidated — that is reported, not hidden.
- **No parameter mutation pass:** 2.0's grid search over ~40 mutations was removed. It was curve-fitting dressed as improvement; measuring the presets honestly is more valuable than searching until one passes.

The gate was **not** loosened — the constants are asserted by a test. What
changed is that the lab now trades what the data actually rewards and stops
trading what it punishes:

1. The six 2.0 pattern families measured PF 0.5–1.1 on daily index bars and
   were removed rather than dressed up.
2. Every short preset measured a **losing** edge (PF 0.2–0.6), so indices are
   traded long-only by default; the audit trail is in each mistakes doc.
3. Fixed R targets were replaced by a 6×ATR chandelier trail: the earlier
   targets cut the trend right tail that this edge depends on.
4. 2.0's 40-candidate improvement search was deleted. Presets are fixed,
   documented hypotheses; validation (holdout + folds) replaces the search.
5. Price history is cached for a day in `db/v2/cache/` with a provenance
   sidecar, so a run no longer re-downloads ten years per family and results
   are reproducible offline.

The 6 strategies that fail the gate are published side by side with the
passers — mostly sample-size failures (17–18 trades) in `MAJOR` on NIFTY and
`PULLBACK` presets that fire too rarely. Their measured failures are visible in
their mistakes docs rather than hidden.

## 13. Known limitations (not hidden)

1. **14 of 20 strategies pass, 5 are validated.** The edge is breakout momentum (PF 1.77–2.35, DD 3.2–4.5%, holdout PF 2.19–4.59). Six presets fail on sample size (17–18 trades) and are reported as such. Profit per trade is real but small in absolute terms — 0.75% risk per trade with ~22 trades a decade is roughly 1%/year unlevered, so the edge is a low-drawdown building block, not a standalone income stream. Evidence is in `strategies/index.json` and each mistakes doc; the gate was never weakened.
2. **Machine-wide Node is still 24.18.0.** REA needs ≥24.22.1: a verified portable Node 24.22.3 is activated through `ZERO_NODE_DIR` (user scope), `run.bat` prepends it, and the MCP bridge connects with 263 tools. The UAC-elevated `winget` upgrade was unavailable (approval timed out) and writing the user PATH key is policy-denied on this machine, so a plain `node -v` in an old shell still reports 24.18.0.
3. **Docker build unverified** (no Docker on this machine). Config reviewed against the actual routes and Next config.
4. **No tick/Level-2 depth**, so whale identification is a footprint heuristic. The module says `exchange_depth_available: False`.
5. **Macro inputs are sourced as of 21 Nov 2026, with dates in each `_source` field:** repo 5.50% (RBI, 7 Oct 2026), Q4-26 oil supply gap −1.65% (IEA 13 Nov / EIA 10 Nov — negative = surplus), FY27 Nifty earnings growth 14% (HSBC / consensus), CPI 6.09% (MoSPI, Oct 2026), policy tone −0.30 (calibrated tightening), insider tone −0.05 (promoters net sold ₹252 Cr in 30 days). `fed_funds_pct` and `acquisition_score` stay `null`/0 because no source was entered — the graph reports the gap instead of guessing. The **rate channel uses the change between snapshots**, so it contributes 0% until a later snapshot or the 4 Dec 2026 MPC moves the rate; the level itself is recorded.
6. **`institutional_signals.json` is populated** with per-symbol, dated, sourced events: FII holding changes for HDFCBANK (+17%), ICICIBANK (+15%), RELIANCE (+17%), AXISBANK (+12%), BAJFINANCE (+18%), ITC (+9%) from the 18 Nov 2026 FII-flow report; a BAJFINANCE promoter purchase of ₹400 Cr net (SEBI filings, 19 Nov 2026); and V2RETAIL bulk-deal churn (20 Nov 2026). 7. **Root `src/` duplicate retained** (reported, not deleted).
8. **`TradingView` direct feed not implemented**; history comes from yfinance through `data/historical.py`, and the MCP bridge is the intended path for external providers.
